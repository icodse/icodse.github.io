Hotel Recommendations

Here are hotel recommendations, sorted by their proximity to the Institut Teknologi Bandung (ITB) campus.

 

NEO Hotel Dipatiukur by ASTON

Hotel Classification: ⭐⭐⭐ 3-Star Hotel
Guest Rating: ⭐ 4.6/5
Category: 🏨 Hotel

Location: Click Here

NEO Hotel Dipatiukur is located in the Dipati Ukur area, very close to the ITB campus. Its location makes it one of the most convenient mid-range options for conference participants.

  • Located close to the ITB campus and the Dago area.

  • Provides modern rooms suitable for short business and conference stays.

  • Surrounded by numerous restaurants, cafés, and convenience stores.

  • A particularly convenient option for participants who prioritize proximity to ITB.

ibis Styles Bandung Grand Central

Hotel Classification: ⭐⭐⭐ 3-Star Hotel
Guest Rating: ⭐ 4.8/5
Category: 🏨 Hotel

Location: Click Here

ibis Styles Bandung Grand Central is a 3-star hotel located directly across from Gedung Sate. It provides a balance between affordability, modern facilities, and convenient access to ITB.

  • Features modern and colorful rooms with contemporary design.

  • Provides free Wi-Fi and an on-site restaurant.

  • Located directly across from Gedung Sate and near Gasibu.

  • Suitable for both business and leisure travelers.

  • Shares the Grand Central complex with Pullman Bandung Grand Central.

House Sangkuriang Bandung

Hotel Classification: ⭐⭐⭐ 3-Star Hotel
Guest Rating: ⭐ 4.5/5
Category: 🏨 Hotel

Location: Click Here

House Sangkuriang is located in the Dago area, close to ITB. It offers a quieter atmosphere compared with hotels located in Bandung's central business district.

  • Located conveniently near ITB and the Dago area.

  • Offers a comfortable and relaxed environment.

  • Features a swimming pool and leisure facilities.

  • Suitable for conference participants looking for accommodation in a quieter neighborhood.

favehotel Premier Cihampelas

Hotel Classification: ⭐⭐⭐ 3-Star Hotel
Guest Rating: ⭐ 4.6/5
Category: 🏨 Hotel

Location: Click Here

favehotel Premier Cihampelas is located in the popular Cihampelas area and provides an affordable accommodation option within convenient reach of ITB.

  • Located near Cihampelas Walk (Ciwalk).

  • Provides easy access to restaurants, shopping, and entertainment.

  • Offers practical accommodation suitable for conference and business travelers.

  • A good choice for participants looking for affordable accommodation near a major commercial area.

De Sofia Hotel Bandung

Hotel Classification: ⭐⭐⭐ 3-Star Hotel
Guest Rating: ⭐ 4.3/5
Category: 🏨 Hotel

Location: Click Here

De Sofia Hotel is located in the Dipati Ukur area, making it another convenient accommodation option for participants attending events at ITB.

  • Located relatively close to the ITB campus.

  • Surrounded by restaurants and local dining options in the Dipati Ukur area.

  • Provides comfortable accommodation at a more affordable price point.

  • Suitable for participants who prioritize proximity and value.

Namin Hotel Bandung

Hotel Classification: ⭐⭐⭐ 3-Star Hotel
Guest Rating: ⭐ 4.2/5
Category: 🏨 Hotel

Location: Click Here

Namin Hotel Bandung is located around the Dipati Ukur and Hasanudin area, providing convenient access to ITB and surrounding university districts.

  • Located close to ITB and the Dipati Ukur area.

  • Convenient access to restaurants, cafés, and local amenities.

  • Offers a practical option for short conference stays.

  • Suitable for participants seeking affordable accommodation within easy reach of the campus.

Here are hotel recommendations, sorted by their proximity to the Institut Teknologi Bandung (ITB) campus. The recommendations include a range of 3-star, 4-star, and 5-star hotels suitable for conference participants.

Four Points by Sheraton Bandung

Hotel Classification: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4-Star Hotel
Guest Rating: ⭐ 4.6/5
Category: 🏨 Hotel

Location: Click Here

Four Points by Sheraton Bandung is located approximately 1.4 km from the ITB main gate. It is a highly convenient choice for conference participants who want to stay close to ITB and the Dago area.

  • Features a refined atmosphere with modern rooms and suites.

  • Amenities include an outdoor swimming pool, fitness center, and on-site dining.

  • Located on Jalan Ir. H. Juanda (Dago), close to many restaurants, cafés, and shops.

Novotel Bandung

Hotel Classification: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4-Star Hotel
Guest Rating: ⭐ 4.6/5
Category: 🏨 Hotel

Location: Click Here

Novotel Bandung is roughly 2.1 km from the ITB campus. It is located in central Bandung, providing convenient access to the university, Bandung railway station, and the Cihampelas area.

  • Offers comfortable and spacious rooms suitable for business travelers.

  • Features a swimming pool, restaurant, fitness facilities, and meeting facilities.

  • Located near shopping, dining, and entertainment destinations.

Pullman Bandung Grand Central

Hotel Classification: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5-Star Hotel
Guest Rating: ⭐ 4.9/5
Category: 🏨 Hotel

Location: Click Here

Pullman Bandung Grand Central is located about 2.2 km from ITB. It is one of Bandung's premium luxury hotels and is positioned directly across from the iconic Gedung Sate.

  • Known for its upscale atmosphere, rooftop bar, and premium spa services.

  • Features an outdoor swimming pool and high-end fitness facilities.

  • Offers extensive meeting and event facilities, making it particularly suitable for conference guests.

  • Its central location provides convenient access to many of Bandung's landmarks.

Hilton Bandung

Hotel Classification: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5-Star Hotel
Guest Rating: ⭐ 4.7/5
Category: 🏨 Hotel

Location: Click Here

Hilton Bandung is approximately 3.1 km from the ITB campus. While slightly farther from the university, it offers premium accommodation and is particularly convenient for participants arriving through Bandung Railway Station.

  • Features a rooftop swimming pool with views of the surrounding mountains.

  • Offers spa facilities, a fitness center, and multiple dining options.

  • Provides large and modern rooms suitable for international and business travelers.

  • Located close to Bandung Railway Station and the 23 Paskal shopping complex.

Crowne Plaza Bandung

Hotel Classification: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5-Star Hotel
Guest Rating: ⭐ 4.8/5
Category: 🏨 Hotel

Location: Click Here

Crowne Plaza Bandung is about 3.5 km from ITB. It is situated in the historic Braga and Asia Afrika area, making it a suitable choice for guests who would also like to explore Bandung's cultural and historical attractions.

  • Offers five-star accommodation designed for both business and leisure travelers.

  • Features a swimming pool, fitness facilities, restaurants, and business facilities.

  • Located near Jalan Braga, Jalan Asia Afrika, and Bandung's central business district.

  • Provides convenient access to many of Bandung's historical landmarks.
